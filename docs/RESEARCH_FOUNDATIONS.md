# Technical foundations and claims

POA uses established integer lending arithmetic, OpenZeppelin StandardMerkleTree-compatible double-hashed leaves, and Solidity claim accounting. It does not claim new cryptography, formal causal execution equivalence, academic endorsement or an official EVMbench score.

The manifest's SHA-256 seal is an integrity checksum, not a signature or proof that its inputs are true. Merkle inclusion proves membership in an owner-approved root, not economic correctness. Historical block hashes and receipts make the demo evidence inspectable, subject to the stated RPC and chain assumptions.

Prior unverified paper citations and security-immunity language have been removed from the active submission material. An independent audit and source-verified research review are prerequisites for stronger claims.
