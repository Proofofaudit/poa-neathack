# Commercial assumptions

No production cost, gross-margin, LTV or customer-demand claims are validated by this repository. Prior numerical margin and run-cost estimates are withdrawn from the submission narrative.

A future business case should measure investigation latency, archive RPC usage, model tokens when enabled, operator review time, storage, audit expense and treasury governance costs. Compare those measured costs with actual protocol incident workflows and customer interviews. The hackathon demo establishes technical feasibility within its stated synthetic scenario only.
