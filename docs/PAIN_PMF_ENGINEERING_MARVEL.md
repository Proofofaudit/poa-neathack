# Product rationale

Oracle-related liquidation disputes combine three jobs: reconstructing what happened, deciding who qualifies under a policy, and distributing compensation. POA demonstrates that workflow with inspectable evidence and restrictive failure behavior.

The intended users are protocol incident responders and treasury governors; beneficiaries are borrowers who qualify under a pre-agreed policy. Customer demand, adoption and willingness to pay are hypotheses requiring interviews and pilots. No product-market-fit or production-readiness claim is made.

See SUBMISSION_GUIDE.md for the demonstration and SECURITY.md for the trust boundary.
